Delete your account

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

Open My Dukan Hisab → More → Plan & Account → Delete my account. Google will ask you to confirm once. After that your account record is erased from our server and you are logged out.

Option 2: by email

Write to arbaab9089@gmail.com from the Gmail address you used to log in, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your record within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your email address, trial start date, plan and plan expiry, and login count stored on our server. This ends your free trial and any paid plan linked to that email, and it cannot be undone.

What is not deleted

Your shop data (items, bills, customers) lives only on your own phone and is not on our server, so you can keep or erase it yourself (More → Settings, or clear the app's data). A Google Drive backup stays in your own Google Drive until you delete it there; you can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
