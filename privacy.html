My Dukan Hisab

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · अंतिम अपडेट: 15 नवंबर 2026

Overview

My Dukan Hisab is an offline billing, stock and Udhaar khata app for small shops. The data you enter (shop details, items, bills, customers) is stored only in your own phone's browser storage. We do not have a copy of it.

Login with Google (optional)

If you log in, the app asks Google for your email address and basic profile. This is used only to start your 14-day free trial, to recognise your plan and to keep one trial per Google account. The same sign-in is used for Google Drive backup.

What our server stores

When you log in, our server (hosted on Cloudflare) stores only: your email address, the date your trial started, your plan and its expiry date, and the time and number of your logins. It never receives your shop name, items, bills, customers, Udhaar balances or any file from your Google Drive. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising. Cloudflare processes it on our behalf as a hosting provider.

Google Drive backup (Pro)

If you choose to back up to Google Drive, the app requests only the "drive.file" permission. With this permission the app can create and read only its own backup file in your Google Drive. It cannot see, change or delete any other file. The backup stays in your own Drive and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Why we keep it, and for how long

We keep your email, trial date and plan so that your trial and plan work again if you change phones or reinstall the app. We keep them for as long as your account exists. You can delete your record yourself inside the app (Plan & Account → Delete my account) or on the account deletion page. Deleting it ends your trial and any plan linked to it.

What stays after you delete your account

So that deleting and logging in again cannot be used to get a new free trial, we keep one anonymous record: a one-way scrambled code (a salted hash) made from your email, and your original trial start date. Your email address itself is not kept, and the code cannot be turned back into your email. This record is used only to stop repeat free trials. You may ask us to remove it too by writing to the contact email below.

Who may use the app

The app is meant for shop owners and staff who are 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has logged in, write to us and we will delete the record.

Your customers' data

Names, phone numbers and Udhaar balances of your customers stay only on your own phone. You, the shop owner, decide how to use and protect them. We do not receive them.

Your consent

Login with Google is optional. By logging in you agree to the use described on this page. You can take back your consent at any time by logging out and deleting your account.

Third-party services

When you scan a barcode, the app may look up the product name on OpenFoodFacts. When you choose, bills and messages are shared through WhatsApp. These services follow their own policies.

Your control

You can log out inside the app, delete your account and server record inside the app (or by email), delete the backup file from your Google Drive at any time, remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or clear the app's data from your browser settings.

Your rights, complaints and data problems

You may ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or to name the people who can see it. Write to ebooksupport07@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will reply within 7 days and complete the request within 30 days. If we are not able to solve your complaint, you may contact the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If we ever learn that your data held on our server was exposed, we will tell the affected users and the authorities as the law requires.
