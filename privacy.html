My Dukan Hisab

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Overview

My Dukan Hisab is an offline billing, stock and Udhaar khata app for small shops. It does not have its own server. The data you enter (shop details, items, bills, customers) is stored only in your own phone's browser storage.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to back up to Google Drive, the app asks you to sign in with Google and requests only the "drive.file" permission. With this permission the app can create and read only its own backup file in your Google Drive. It cannot see, change or delete any other file. The backup is stored in your own Drive account and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. We have no access to your bills, customers or Google account. Google may process your sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Third-party services

When you scan a barcode, the app may look up the product name on OpenFoodFacts. When you choose, bills and messages are shared through WhatsApp. These services follow their own policies.

Your control

You can delete the backup file from your Google Drive at any time, remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or clear the app's data from your browser settings.
