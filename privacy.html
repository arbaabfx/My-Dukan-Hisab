My Dukan Hisab

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Overview

My Dukan Hisab is an offline billing, stock and Udhaar khata app for small shops. The data you enter (shop details, items, bills, customers) is stored only in your own phone's browser storage. We do not have a copy of it.

Login with Google (optional)

If you log in, the app asks Google for your email address and basic profile. This is used only to start your 14-day free trial, to recognise your plan and to keep one trial per Google account. The same sign-in is used for Google Drive backup.

What our server stores

When you log in, our server (hosted on Cloudflare) stores only: your email address, the date your trial started, your plan and its expiry date, and the time and number of your logins. It never receives your shop name, items, bills, customers, Udhaar balances or any file from your Google Drive. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising. Cloudflare processes it on our behalf as a hosting provider.

Google Drive backup (Pro)

If you choose to back up to Google Drive, the app requests only the "drive.file" permission. With this permission the app can create and read only its own backup file in your Google Drive. It cannot see, change or delete any other file. The backup stays in your own Drive and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Why we keep it, and for how long

We keep your email, trial date and plan so that your trial and plan work again if you change phones or reinstall the app. You can ask us to delete your record at any time by writing to the address below; deleting it ends your trial and any plan linked to it.

Third-party services

When you scan a barcode, the app may look up the product name on OpenFoodFacts. When you choose, bills and messages are shared through WhatsApp. These services follow their own policies.

Your control

You can log out inside the app, ask us to delete your server record, delete the backup file from your Google Drive at any time, remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or clear the app's data from your browser settings.
